# MiniMax-M3 MXFP8 B300 component capacity update

## Result

The latest B300 component sweeps select P C20 and D C72 under the production
SLA.

| Stage | Sweet point | Per-engine QPS | 36-GPU QPS | 36-GPU TPM | SLA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P | C20 | 7.374 | 66.37 | **317.11M input TPM** | P50 TTFT 2.61 s |
| D | C72 | 7.642 | 68.78 | **2.48M output TPM** | P50 TPOT 11.86 ms |

The detailed D boundary measurements are:

| Concurrency | Request QPS | Output tok/s | P50 TPOT | P50 TTFT | Acceptance | QPS CV |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 64 | 7.298 | 4,379 | 9.54 ms | 3.15 s | 53.10% | 0.50% |
| **72** | **7.642** | **4,585** | **11.86 ms** | **1.88 s** | **53.15%** | **0.24%** |

C72 is the highest stable point in this sweep that passes the D-side
production SLA of P50 TPOT below 16.6 ms. All three trials completed without
failed requests. C80 and higher were unstable and are excluded from the
capacity calculation.

## Updated 72-GPU capacity

For the requested fixed deployment of **36 P GPUs + 36 D GPUs**, each side has
nine TP4 engines:

```text
P capacity = 9 * 7.374 = 66.37 QPS
D capacity = 9 * 7.642 = 68.78 QPS
effective end-to-end capacity = min(66.37, 68.78) = 66.37 QPS
```

PD TPM is the sum of input tokens processed by P and output tokens generated by
D. The input tokens received by D must not be counted a second time:

```text
P input TPM  = 317.11M
D output TPM =   2.48M
total logical TPM = 317.11M + 2.48M = 319.59M
```

At the observed **89.9% prefix-cache hit rate**, P's fresh-compute input rate is
approximately **32.00M TPM**.

The fixed 36P/36D deployment is P-bound, with about 2.41 QPS of aggregate
D-side headroom. This remains a component-capacity estimate, not a replacement
for a sustained open-loop 10-minute PD qualification.

- Hardware: B300; nine TP4 P engines and nine TP4 D engines in the 72-GPU
  projection.

  draft=3, synthetic acceptance `[0.7, 0.5, 0.4]`.

  C72. This establishes the component compute envelope; it is not an
